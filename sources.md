# Source verification for 24 November 2026

The edition covers events that happened or were first reported on 24 November 2026 in any time zone. Each story in [news.json](news.json) and [entry.png](entry.png) has a dated development on 24 November.

## Yemen displacement and vaccination access

**24 November in New York.** UN spokesperson Stéphane Dujarric reported that fighting and insecurity had displaced more than 364,000 people across Yemen since early August. He also said insecurity kept about 350,000 children in Taiz governorate from a national measles and rubella vaccination campaign. The story covers the updated UN figures, which describe cumulative displacement. The original Taiz subtotal of about 145,000 was removed because its geographic scope could not be confirmed from the sources used here. [Arab News reporting on the Tuesday UN briefing](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/350000-yemeni-children-in-besieged-taiz-miss-out-on-measles-vaccine-as-war-hinders-un-access-3013932) · [24 November coverage from Yemen Children Platform](https://yemenschildren.net/en/350%2C000-Children-in-Taiz-Missed-Measles-Vaccination-Due-to-Insecurity)

## DR Congo evacuation and UNICEF findings

**24 November in Ituri.** MONUSCO says its peacekeepers helped evacuate Ebola-response workers from Akwe on 24 November. AP quotes WHO incident manager Catherine Smallwood confirming that evacuees reached Bunia that Tuesday after three full days sheltering from fighting. [MONUSCO operation report](https://monusco.unmissions.org/en/news/ituri-la-monusco-renforce-ses-interventions-pour-proteger-les-civils-face-a-la) · [Associated Press interview with WHO](https://apnews.com/article/ff1890d63ca7f3c3a8beeb127727e5cd)

**24 November in Geneva.** UNICEF separately reported that nearly six in ten women and adolescent girls surveyed said violence or abuse had begun or increased since the Ebola outbreak started. The figure applies to survey respondents. [UNICEF briefing](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/families-are-under-enormous-pressure-because-ebola-and-women-and-girls-are-bearing)

## Senegal's AFCON title

**24 November in Lausanne.** CAS set aside the CAF Appeal Board's 17 March 2026 decision awarding Morocco a 3-0 win by forfeit and reinstated the 11 February disciplinary decision. That confirmed Senegal's 1-0 extra-time win in the 18 January 2026 final and its 2025 AFCON title. CAS held that the restart was within the referee's authority and that the cited forfeit rules apply when a match is permanently called off. The dated development is the award. [CAS media release](https://www.tas-cas.org/generated/assets/lists/dceab111-07bc-435f-b5f9-de88eff9db72/CAS%20Media%20Release_12295_decision.pdf) · [CAF statement](https://www.cafonline.com/inside-caf/news/caf-media-statement-on-the-court-of-arbitration-for-sport-cas-award-in-the-matter-concerning-the-totalenergies-caf-africa-cup-of-nations-morocco-2025-final) · [The Guardian coverage of the final and award](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/nov/24/afcon-verdict-senegal-morocco-africa-cup-of-nations-champions-court-ruling-cas)

## Shenzhou-24 crew announcement

**24 November at 09:00 Beijing time.** The China Manned Space Agency announced commander and pilot Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi, and Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali. CMSA said Ali would be the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong. The card uses the crew announcement as the dated development. [CMSA press conference](https://www.cmse.gov.cn/xwzx/202611/t20261124_57876.html) · [Xinhua crew announcement](https://english.news.cn/20261124/c857a5e3dc2c4a139a843a5801b8f70b/c.html)

CMSA records liftoff at 08:18 Beijing time on 25 November, equivalent to 00:18 UTC on 25 November and 19:18 EST on 24 November. Docking was at 11:56 Beijing time on 25 November, equivalent to 03:56 UTC, and the crew entered the station later that day. [CMSA mission record](https://en.cmse.gov.cn/missions/shenzhou24/) · [Xinhua station arrival report](https://english.news.cn/20261125/8bde8abe770f4a329ae7a96ce945181d/c.html)
